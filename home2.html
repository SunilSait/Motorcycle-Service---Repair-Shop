Rider FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our service standards, OEM parts, and warranty.

How often should I get my motorcycle serviced?
For daily commuting motorcycles, we recommend a routine service every 3,000 to 5,000 km or every 6 months. For high-performance superbikes (KTM Duke 390, Ducati, Ninja, Triumph), synthetic oil and filter checks should be done every 3,000 km or before aggressive track sessions.
Do you provide genuine OEM parts and warranty on repairs?
Yes, 100%. We source exclusively from authorized manufacturers (Yamaha Genuine, Brembo, Motul, NGK, D.I.D) and provide an unconditional 90-day parts and labor warranty on every major overhaul.
Can I get live service updates while my bike is at the shop?
Absolutely! Our lead mechanics send photo and video inspection reports directly to your WhatsApp before replacing any component, ensuring complete transparency with zero surprise charges.
Do you offer motorcycle pickup and drop service?
Yes, we offer doorstep flatbed motorcycle pickup and drop services across the city. You can select pickup when booking online or by calling our direct hotline.
